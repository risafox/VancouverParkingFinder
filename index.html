Busyness
There is no public block-by-block occupancy feed. Rates are the best signal: the City raises a meter's rate by $1/hr in areas that were at least 85% full, so a high rate means a busy block. Spots also show when a ticket hotspot is within 150 m.
Lots
Off-street lots come from EasyPark and other listings plus OpenStreetMap. Rates and hours are not in the data: confirm on arrival.
Permit Zones
RPO — Resident Parking Only. Reserved for residents with no permits issued — obey posted signs.
RPP — Residential Parking Permit required during restricted hours.
VRPP — Vancouver Resident Parking Permit, a citywide permit program.
Tickets
Blocks with the most parking tickets recently (the year is shown when you tap a dot). Larger dot = more tickets. Locations are 100-block level.

Parking is free right now

Meters are free from 10pm to 9am and charge again from 9am. The dots on the map are grey while meters are off. To see prices for later, set the Arrive time under the search box.

How to use this map

  1. 1
    Search for where you're going
    Type an address or place name. Pick one of the suggestions and the map lists parking nearby, cheapest first. Tap a result to light it up on the map, then tap Open in Maps for directions.
  2. 2
    Check the shaded zones
    Shaded areas are residential permit zones. You need a permit to park there during the posted hours, so tap one to read the details.
    RPO RPP VRPP
  3. 3
    Or just explore
    Every dot is a metered spot. Tap one to see its price and time limit.
    $1.50/hr or less up to $3.00 $3+ (busy areas)
Meters run 9am–10pm every day and are free outside those hours. Most are pay-by-phone.
